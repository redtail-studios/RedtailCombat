##/what-we-do

An end-to-end intelligence loop. Six stages. Agents do four of them. Humans do one. Everyone learns.

  1. sense · agents scan forums, stores, reviews, creator content
  2. synthesize · strategy agents turn signals into game concepts
  3. simulate · prototype agents + AI-vs-AI playtests for balance
  4. build · human PM-led team picks the strongest concept and ships it
  5. launch · live-ops, marketing, continuous updates
  6. learn · performance data retrains the engine for the next pick
